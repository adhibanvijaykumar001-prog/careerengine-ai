How CareerEngine Works

Step-by-step intelligence pipeline overview

1. Skill Ingestion & Vectorization

When you select technologies or paste your resume, Agent 1 normalizes them into a multi-dimensional binary feature vector representing your capabilities.

2. Decision Tree Classification & What-If ROI

A Scikit-Learn Decision Tree evaluates your vector against industrial benchmark standards, computing instant What-If career return projections for each missing competency.

3. Regional Vacancy & Placement Pass

Agent 3 and 4 synthesize metric-driven STAR bullets for placement resumes, link accredited IBM SkillsBuild coursework, and filter active tech centers in your target city.
